but carrying it around everywhere, complaining your life stinks?
that's ridicul‎ous. junk in, junk out － what did you expect?

time to clean up.

processing our experiences is done directly or indirectly.

if you don't consciously decide what to believe,
‎
**someone else will decide for you.**

instead of basing assumptions on your own life,
you're drip-fed other people's neuroses, trauma & resentment.

usually turning grey.
